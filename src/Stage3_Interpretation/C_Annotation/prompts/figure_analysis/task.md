# TASK

Look at the figure image, then fill every field:
- visible_observations: up to 8 short observations of what the image shows, each naming its panel; image only, never the caption.
- caption_context: up to 4 short notes of what the caption adds (conditions, labels you cannot read in the image); empty when it adds nothing.
- interpretation: 1-3 sentences on what the figure shows, from the observations.
- relation: supports, contradicts, not_clearly_addressed or cannot_assess, judged against the SUB-CLAIM only.
- relation_rationale: 1-2 sentences linking the observations (by panel) to the sub-claim.
- panels_referenced: the panel letters the observations use (e.g. ["A", "C"]); empty for a single panel.
- assay_seen: the assay visible in the image, or empty.
- system_seen: the species / cell type / tissue labelled in the image, or empty.
- image_quality: ok, partial_crop (part of the figure is cut off) or illegible.
- confidence: high, medium or low.
